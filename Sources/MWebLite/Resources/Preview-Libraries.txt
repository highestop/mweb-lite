Optional local renderers:
An existing MWeb Pro installation can supply its installed third-party MathJax,
Mermaid 10.2.4, Prism and ECharts scripts at runtime. MWeb Lite does not copy or
redistribute any MWeb application files. CommonMark/GFM rendering works offline
without MWeb. Missing optional renderers leave mathematical notation or diagram
source visible. ECharts accepts JSON5 data options only; functions are not run.
